Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms carefully before registering as a tutor.

1

Registration Fee

A nominal registration fee is applicable for profile activation. This is a one-time fee and is non-refundable.

2

Demo Class Policy

Tutors must conduct a demo class before starting regular sessions. The first demo class is free for parents to evaluate teaching quality.

3

Punctuality & Commitment

Tutors are expected to maintain punctuality and complete the agreed number of classes. Prior notice is required for any schedule changes.

4

Academic Responsibility

Tutors are responsible for student progress and must provide regular feedback. Study materials and homework should be provided as needed.

5

Code of Conduct

Professional behavior is mandatory. Any misconduct or violation of trust will result in immediate termination from our platform.

6

Payment Terms

Payments are processed monthly after successful completion of classes. Commission structure will be communicated during onboarding.
